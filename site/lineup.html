Lineup · beta

Set your home-screen lineup

Stremio lists home rows add-on by add-on, in the order they're installed. Lineup reorders them and hides the rows you don't want, and the change follows your account to every device, Google TV included.

Connect your Stremio account

Signed up with Google, Apple or Facebook? You don't have a Stremio password; use an auth key below.

Use an auth key instead

On web.stremio.com (signed in), open the browser console and run JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("profile")).auth.key, then paste the result here.

How it works

  • Everything runs in this browser tab. Your login goes only to Stremio's own API (api.strem.io) and is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Nothing changes until you press Save to Stremio, and every save downloads a backup of your add-on list first.
  • Hidden rows are changed in Stremio's saved copy of that add-on. If the add-on updates itself later, its rows may come back; just run Lineup again.
  • “Continue Watching” always stays at the top. Stremio doesn't let add-ons move it.

Search keeps working

Rows that power search, like Cinemeta's Popular, aren't deleted when you hide them. They're switched to search-only, the way Stremio's own YouTube add-on works, so they leave the home screen and Discover but still answer searches. Rows marked still searchable are hidden this way.
